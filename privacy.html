SkyCast Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

What SkyCast keeps

When you make a SkyCast account, your email and your SkyCast settings (like saved cities, units, theme and notification choices) are stored with Google Firebase so they can follow you to any device you sign in on. Passwords are handled by Firebase and SkyCast never sees them. Only you can read your saved data. SkyCast does not sell or share it.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google tells SkyCast your name and email address. SkyCast uses them only to show your account on your device. SkyCast does not get your Google password or anything else in your Google account.

Location and weather

If you let SkyCast use your location, it is used to look up your weather from the US National Weather Service and other weather services. Your location is not stored anywhere except on your device.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Account → Delete account to erase your account and its saved data. Clearing your browser's site data also removes everything.
